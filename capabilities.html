Practice area 01

Protective Operations & Security Programs

Protection is a planning discipline before it is a physical one. We design and run protective programs built on threat-informed advance work, disciplined logistics, and clear command relationships — the same architecture used to move principals through high-threat environments, applied to the personnel, facilities, and events an agency cannot afford to lose.

Services

  • Executive and dignitary protection
  • Protective advance operations and route planning
  • Protective intelligence and threat monitoring
  • Physical security assessment and design
  • Security program development and governance
  • Access control and post-order design
  • Special event and high-visibility security planning
  • High-risk travel and duty-of-care programs

How we are evaluated

Programs are delivered with written CONOPS, documented post orders, defined command relationships, and an incident-reporting architecture that produces a record the customer owns.

Practice area 02

Training, Exercises & Readiness

Training is where an agency’s readiness is either built or assumed. We build it. Curricula are designed against the customer’s actual threat picture, delivered by instructors with documented operational credentials, and instrumented so that improvement is measured rather than asserted.

Services

  • Tactical and protective operations training
  • Active-threat response and workforce survivability
  • Security awareness and insider-threat awareness
  • High-risk travel and hostile-environment preparation
  • Tabletop, functional, and full-scale exercise design and control
  • Crisis management and incident command training
  • SOP, curriculum, and lesson-plan development
  • Instructor development and train-the-trainer

Differentiators

  • Verifiable instructor pedigree. Every instructor’s operational credentials are documented and produced on request.
  • Formal safety program. Written safety plan, range and scenario protocols, medical coverage standard, and a zero-mishap governance model on every iteration.
  • Outcome instrumentation. Pre/post assessment, competency scoring, and after-action reporting in a format a COR can lift directly into a performance narrative.
  • Surge responsiveness. Documented mobilization timelines and a bench structure built to answer short-notice taskings.

Practice area 03

Risk, Resilience & Emergency Management

Resilience is a plan that survives contact. We assess what can actually go wrong, build the continuity and response architecture to absorb it, and then test it — because an untested plan is a document, not a capability.

Services

  • Operational risk and vulnerability assessment
  • Physical security and facility risk assessment
  • Continuity of operations (COOP) planning
  • Crisis management and incident command structure design
  • Emergency preparedness and response planning
  • Protective intelligence programs
  • Vendor and third-party risk review
  • Organizational readiness assessment
  • Policy, SOP, and governance development

How we are evaluated

Assessments are delivered with prioritized, costed findings and a tested response architecture — not a binder that has never met a scenario.

Practice area 04

Facility & Operations Support

Missions run on facilities that work. We provide the operational management, maintenance coordination, logistics, and program discipline that keep installations, facilities, and infrastructure mission-ready — and we do it with the same documentation standard we apply to protective work.

Services

  • Facility management and operations support
  • Preventive and corrective maintenance program management
  • Infrastructure readiness and condition assessment
  • Vendor and subcontractor management
  • Logistics coordination, supply support, and asset accountability
  • Program and project management, PMO support
  • Schedule, quality, and performance reporting
  • Continuity and contingency planning for facility operations

How we are evaluated

This is the volume and scale lane — the widest set-aside pipeline and the natural landing zone for subcontracted work, delivered to the same documented standard.
