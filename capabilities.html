Four practice areas. One operating standard.

Three security disciplines and one operations-support lane. Each states what we do, who it serves, what the customer receives, and the codes it maps to — so a contracting officer can place us against a solicitation quickly.

Practice areas

01

Protective Program Advisory & Assessment

Physical security and vulnerability assessment, protective-program design and governance, advance methodology, protective intelligence, and duty-of-care standards. We assess and design — we do not staff protective details.

NAICS 541690 · 541990  |  PSC R408 · R425

Training, Exercises & Readiness

Custom course design against the customer’s threat picture, protective and active-threat instruction, tabletop through full-scale exercises, and after-action review — readiness built and measured, not assumed.

NAICS 611430 · 611519  |  PSC U-series

Explore capability → 03

Risk, Resilience & Emergency Management

Operational risk and vulnerability assessment, continuity of operations, emergency action and crisis-management planning, incident-command design, and exercise validation — tested before it is needed.

NAICS 541690 · 541990  |  PSC R-series

Explore capability → 04

Facility & Operations Support

Operational management, coordination, readiness, and reporting for mission-critical facilities under NAICS 561210. Apex holds the program discipline; licensed trades are performed by qualified providers.

NAICS 561210  |  PSC M-series

What we self-perform, and what we team

Advisory, assessment, program design, training, and program management are self-performed. Guard-force operations, investigations, alarm and security-system installation, and construction or trades are delivered through licensed providers and specialist subcontractors under our management.
