Accessibility.

Our commitment to an accessible website, and how to reach us with an issue.

Our commitment

Apex Gama Defense Solutions is working to improve accessibility in alignment with generally recognized web-accessibility practices, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. We design for keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, descriptive alternative text, and clear heading structure.

Testing

We test with automated accessibility tooling as part of our build process. [ERIC TO CONFIRM: whether formal, third-party WCAG conformance testing has been completed; if not, this statement should not claim formal conformance.]

Known limitations

Contact us

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site, please let us know and we will work to provide the information you need in an accessible format.
