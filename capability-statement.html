Company Overview

Apex Gama Solutions is a professional services company supporting government agencies, prime contractors, and mission-driven organizations through operational support, facilities and infrastructure support, logistics, program and project management, training, and advisory and risk services. Our approach combines disciplined execution, structured planning, and practical operational experience to help clients improve readiness, efficiency, and organizational performance — delivered with small-business agility and enterprise-level professionalism, and built around measurable outcomes and documented accountability.
