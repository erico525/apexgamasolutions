Positioning

Apex Gama Defense Solutions is a founder-led security, readiness, and operational-support company. We design, assess, and strengthen protection, training, and resilience programs for government agencies, prime contractors, and mission-driven organizations.

Core competencies

  • Protective program advisory and physical security assessment
  • Training, exercises, and readiness
  • Risk, resilience, and continuity of operations
  • Facility and operations readiness and program support

Differentiators

  • Founder with more than 17 years across special operations, protective operations, maritime security, and security-program leadership
  • Senior accountability held close to delivery; specialist capacity added against each engagement’s requirements
  • Documented, defensible work products — assessments, plans, SOPs, exercise and after-action reports
  • Clear scope boundaries: advisory, assessment, design, and training self-performed; regulated and licensed work delivered through qualified licensed providers

Representative deliverables

  • Security and vulnerability assessments and prioritized findings matrices
  • Protective-program frameworks, governance matrices, and SOP suites
  • Exercise plans, training evaluations, and after-action reports
  • Continuity and emergency action plans and implementation roadmaps

Representative deliverables describe the work products Apex produces; they are not a claim of specific completed government contracts. [ERIC TO PROVIDE: any past-performance references cleared for release.]
