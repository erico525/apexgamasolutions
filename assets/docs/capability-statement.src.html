Founder-led security, readiness, and continuity for government missions — protective programs designed, assessed, and strengthened to withstand scrutiny.

Apex Gama Defense Solutions is a founder-led security, readiness, and operational-support company. We design, assess, and strengthen protection, training, and resilience programs for government agencies, prime contractors, and mission-driven organizations — with senior accountability held close to delivery.

Core competencies

  • Protective program advisory & assessment — physical-security and vulnerability assessment, protective concepts, governance, and SOP suites.
  • Training, exercises & readiness — curriculum design, instruction, tabletop through full-scale exercises, and after-action review.
  • Risk & resilience — risk and threat analysis, continuity of operations, and emergency action planning.
  • Facility & operations support — program management, readiness, and coordination across sites and providers.

Differentiators

  • Founder with 17+ years across special operations, protective operations, maritime security, and security-program leadership.
  • Programs managed across 35+ international locations with a documented zero-incident record.
  • Senior accountability close to delivery; vetted specialist capacity added against each engagement’s requirements.
  • Documented, defensible work products — assessments, plans, SOPs, and exercise and after-action reports.

Representative deliverables

  • Security and vulnerability assessments with prioritized findings matrices.
  • Protective-program frameworks, governance matrices, and SOP suites.
  • Exercise plans, training evaluations, and after-action reports.
  • Continuity and emergency action plans with implementation roadmaps.
