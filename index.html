Who we are

Section 01

The people who plan the work have done the work.

Apex Gama Defense Solutions was formed to bring operator-grade discipline to security, training, and continuity programs in the public sector. Our founder spent more than 15 years running protection and readiness operations where the cost of a failure was measured in lives, not line items.

That is the standard we build to. Programs are documented before they are staffed. Safety architecture precedes execution. Outcomes are instrumented so that performance is provable — to your leadership, to a COR, and in a CPARS review.

How we deliver

An operating model designed to be graded.

Federal performance is not judged on effort. It is judged in CPARS, against a QASP, by a COR who needs evidence. We build backwards from that.

Every engagement runs on the same framework: scope defined in writing, responsibilities assigned by name, safety architecture established before day one, and outcomes instrumented so that performance produces a record — not an assertion.
