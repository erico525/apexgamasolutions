How we work

Apex Gama designs, assesses, and trains. Licensed protective delivery, where required, is performed by licensed providers.

Working with us

Three ways to engage.

01

As a prime contractor

Direct advisory, assessment, design, and training we self-perform, at a scale matched to a founder-led specialist firm. Larger, labor-intensive requirements are pursued through teaming.

02

As a subcontractor

Defined-scope support to primes who need a dependable small-business partner with clear scope discipline. For primes →

03

As a teaming partner

Joint pursuit and delivery where our security, training, and readiness capability completes a partner’s offering on a larger requirement.

What we self-perform, and what we team

Advisory, assessment, program design, training, and program management are self-performed. Guard-force operations, investigations, alarm and security-system installation, and construction or trades are delivered through licensed providers and specialist subcontractors.

Procurement FAQ

Common questions.

Is Apex registered in SAM?

Yes. UEI LN8SWKYL8X81, CAGE 22VC7, registration active. The legal name for a contract or subcontract is Apex Gama Defense Solutions LLC, a California limited liability company.

Can Apex serve as a prime contractor?

Yes, for requirements within our practice areas at a scale matched to a founder-led specialist firm. Larger, labor-intensive requirements are pursued through teaming.

Does Apex provide armed security or guard-force staffing?

No. Apex provides advisory, assessment, program design, and training. Guard-force operations and other licensed services are delivered through licensed providers.

Can Apex support proposal development?

Yes — technical approach, staffing inputs, safety and management sections, and risk identification. See For prime contractors.

Can Apex mobilize outside California?

We evaluate geographic requirements against current availability. [ERIC TO PROVIDE: states in which Apex can currently deliver, and any limits.]

Does Apex work through licensed partners?

Yes. Regulated services and skilled trades are delivered through qualified licensed providers and specialist subcontractors under our management.

How is key-personnel experience presented?

Where permitted by the solicitation, evaluators may consider relevant experience and past performance associated with key personnel, predecessor companies, and related entities under FAR 15.305. Corporate past performance and key-personnel experience are not automatically interchangeable.

How does Apex handle sensitive information?

Do not send classified, controlled unclassified (CUI), export-controlled, or personal information through the website. After initial contact, we will provide an appropriate method for exchanging requirement documents.
