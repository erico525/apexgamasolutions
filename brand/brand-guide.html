Brand Guide
v1.0

The visual identity of Apex Gama Defense Solutions — a disciplined, institutional system built for longevity and for reuse across the website, capability statements, proposals, presentations, letterhead, business cards, and social media.

The mark

The Apex Datum

An ascending peak — the apex: summit, precision, forward progress — with an ochre inner peak, resting on a horizontal datum line: the surveyor's and engineer's reference, signaling structure and foundation. It reads as reliability and disciplined forward motion, with none of the eagle/shield/star/flag clichés of defense branding.

Minimum size: mark no smaller than 24 px on screen / 0.3 in in print. Clear space: at least the width of the datum line on all sides.

Logo system

Color palette

A deep institutional neutral does the work; a single restrained ochre accent — the surveyor's mark — is used sparingly for rules, highlights, and small marks. No patriotic red-white-blue; no tactical black-and-olive.

Typography

Display & headings — Source Serif 4
Operational discipline for government missions.
Body & UI — IBM Plex Sans
Structured, accountable delivery for government, infrastructure, and mission-driven organizations.
Labels & data — IBM Plex Mono
NAICS 541690 · CAGE PENDING · UEI IN PROCESS

All three are free, open-source families (Google Fonts). Source Serif carries editorial authority; Plex Sans is the neutral workhorse; Plex Mono signals the technical, procurement-literate voice.

Usage

Do

  • Give the logo generous clear space.
  • Use the reversed (white) logo on ink/slate backgrounds only.
  • Keep ochre as an accent — rules, small marks, one highlighted word.
  • Pair the serif for headings with Plex Sans for body.
  • Use the mono family for codes, labels, and data.

Don't

  • Recolor the logo or fill large areas with ochre.
  • Stretch, rotate, or add effects/shadows to the mark.
  • Place the dark logo on a dark background (use the reversed one).
  • Introduce patriotic or tactical colors and imagery.
  • Present "DBA" as part of the public brand.

Naming

Public brand (everywhere): Apex Gama Defense Solutions.
Legal entity (footer, legal, contracting docs only): Apex Gama Defense Solutions LLC.
"Gama" is intentional — never change it to "Gamma," and never present "DBA" publicly.
