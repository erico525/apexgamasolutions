An organization built around discipline.

Apex Gama Solutions supports the operation, sustainment, and program execution of complex government and infrastructure work. This is who we are and how we hold ourselves accountable.

Overview

Section 01

We support government, infrastructure, and mission-driven organizations as a disciplined operational partner.

The company was formed to bring structure and accountability to operational support — the sustained, unglamorous work that keeps public missions running. We measure ourselves by reliability, not by promises.

Mission

Apex Gama Solutions delivers disciplined operational, technical, and professional support services that enable government and mission-driven organizations to operate more safely, efficiently, and effectively. We are committed to providing reliable execution, practical expertise, and responsive partnership in support of critical missions.

Vision

To become a trusted national government contractor recognized for operational excellence, technical capability, and unwavering integrity, delivering scalable solutions that strengthen mission readiness, infrastructure resilience, and organizational performance.

Operating principles

How we work, regardless of the engagement.

01

Structure before speed

Clear scope, defined responsibilities, and a common framework precede execution. Structure is what makes delivery predictable.

02

Accountability in the open

Responsibility is assigned and visible. Progress is measured against commitments, not intentions.

03

Precision over promotion

We scope what we can perform and say so plainly. We do not overstate capability to win attention.

04

Built to endure

Systems, documentation, and standards are designed to outlast any single engagement or individual.

Positioning

Clear about what we are.

Precision starts with an honest definition of scope. We would rather be exact than expansive.

A

What we are

A disciplined operational-support partner — organized to plan, sustain, coordinate, and execute government and infrastructure work with measured accountability.

B

What we are not

Not a general consulting firm and not a security company. We do not position ourselves outside the work we can perform, and we do not fabricate experience.

Leadership

Led by its founder.

Apex Gama Solutions is led by its founder, with direct accountability for the company’s standards and delivery.

EM

CEO & Founder

Eric M. Marks

Eric M. Marks is the CEO and Founder of Apex Gama Solutions. He brings more than 15 years of security and operations leadership in high-risk, high-stakes environments — including oversight of multimillion-dollar budgets and global programs across 35-plus international locations, with a consistent record of disciplined execution and zero-incident delivery.

His work centers on risk management, program execution, crisis response, and building scalable operational frameworks with clear governance and accountability. A background spanning military special operations and private-sector executive leadership shapes the measured, disciplined approach he brings to supporting government and mission-driven organizations.
