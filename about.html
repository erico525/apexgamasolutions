Apex Gama Defense Solutions supports protection, readiness, and continuity for government and critical-infrastructure missions. This is who we are and how we hold ourselves accountable.

Overview

Section 01

We are a security and readiness firm. We say so plainly.

Most operational-support contractors treat security as a staffing category. We treat it as the discipline. Protection, training, and resilience are what we do, what our founder has done for more than 17 years, and what every process in this company is built around.

Where our capability reaches, we scope precisely. Where it does not, we say so and step back. Both halves of that sentence are load-bearing.

Mission

Apex Gama Defense Solutions designs, assesses, and strengthens the protective, training, and resilience programs that enable government and mission-driven organizations to operate safely, continuously, and under pressure. We are accountable for outcomes, not activity.

Vision

To be the small government contractor that agencies name when the requirement is hard, the consequence is real, and the performance record has to hold up.

Operating principles

How we work, regardless of the engagement.

01

Structure before speed

Scope, responsibility, and safety architecture are established in writing before anyone deploys. Structure is what makes delivery predictable and performance provable.

02

Accountability by name

Every task has an owner. Progress is measured against written commitments, and the record is available to the customer at any time — not at closeout.

03

Zero-mishap by design

Safety is engineered into program design, not appended to it. In protective and tactical work, a mishap is not a metric. It is a failure of planning.

04

Evidence over assertion

Outcomes are instrumented so performance produces a record a COR can cite and a CPARS entry can rest on.

05

Built to endure

Systems, documentation, and standards are designed to outlast any single engagement, contract, or individual.

Positioning

Clear about what we are.

Discipline about what we will not bid is what makes us credible on what we will.

A

What we are

An operator-led security and readiness contractor: protective program advisory, training and exercises, risk and resilience advisory, and the facility and operations support that sustains them.

B

What we are not

Not a body shop and not a generalist consultancy. We do not sell headcount, and we do not bid requirements we are not built to perform.

Leadership

Led by its founder.

A new entity wins on the credibility of its key personnel. Ours is at the center of every engagement.

EM

CEO & Founder

Eric M. Marks

Eric M. Marks founded Apex Gama Defense Solutions to deliver elite operational expertise to government security, maritime security, training, and continuity programs. A former operator in the Israeli Navy’s elite special operations force, he has spent more than 17 years leading complex security, intelligence, and crisis-response operations worldwide, managing multimillion-dollar programs across more than 35 international locations with a zero-incident record.

His experience spans maritime security, counter-terrorism, executive protection, protective intelligence, critical infrastructure, and strategic security operations, combining frontline operational experience with executive-level program leadership.
